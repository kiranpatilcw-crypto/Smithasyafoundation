About Us

Striving to Create Joy in Every Life

Smithasya Foundation is a community initiative working to support marginalized sections of society. We believe that even a small act of kindness can transform someone's life.

Our dedicated volunteers regularly conduct social initiatives in Jalgaon and surrounding areas. You are welcome to join our hands in this noble cause.
